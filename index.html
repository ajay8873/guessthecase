How to Play DoctorAJ

Your goal is to guess the correct medical diagnosis in 6 attempts or fewer.

  • 1
    You start with the Initial Presentation outlining the patient's demographics, history, and chief complaint.
  • 2
    To make a guess, type in the search box and **you MUST select a diagnosis from the dropdown list of suggestions**. (Guesses not selected from the dropdown won't submit).
  • 3
    Click Submit to test your diagnosis. To reveal the next clue without guessing, **leave the search box blank and click Submit to skip the turn**.
  • 4
    Reveal up to 6 clues. Correctly identify the disease to win, read the full clinical review, and sync study tags straight to Anki!
